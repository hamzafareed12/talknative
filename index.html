Questions

Common questions

What is TalkNative?

An AI speaking practice app on the web. You talk out loud with an AI Partner that answers by voice and corrects your grammar and wording as you go, across eight modules from IELTS to everyday conversation.

Is it free?

Every module has a free tier, so you can try it before you decide. Paid plans give longer practice. The TalkNative pricing page shows what each one includes.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser on desktop or mobile. You only need a microphone.

Can practice guarantee my IELTS band or my visa?

No. The examiner and the visa officer decide. Practice makes your answers clearer and more confident, which improves your chances.

Which languages can I practise?

57, from English, Spanish, French and German to Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi and Urdu. See all the languages.
