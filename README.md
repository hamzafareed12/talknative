**TalkNative** is a voice-first practice app for the web. You speak out loud with an AI Partner, it answers by voice like a real person, and every mistake appears under your sentence with the better version. It covers IELTS preparation, visa interview practice and everyday conversation in 57 languages.

## The modules

| Module | What you practise |
|---|---|
| [IELTS](https://www.talknative.io/ielts) | Practice tests for all four papers with band scores: a [Speaking mock test](https://www.talknative.io/ielts/speaking) with an AI examiner, a [Writing checker](https://www.talknative.io/ielts-writing), [Listening](https://www.talknative.io/ielts-listening) and [Reading](https://www.talknative.io/ielts-reading) tests, and a [full mock test](https://www.talknative.io/ielts-complete) |
| [Visa Interview](https://www.talknative.io/visa) | A mock visa interview with an AI consular officer for 25 embassies, with every answer reviewed afterwards |
| [Call Mode](https://www.talknative.io/call) | Real-time AI phone calls with characters who remember you |
| [Real World Scenarios](https://www.talknative.io/scenarios) | Roleplay speaking practice: job interviews, a call center shift, the doctor, the airport |
| [Debate Arena](https://www.talknative.io/debate) | Argue a motion against an AI rival, then a judge scores the round |
| [Build Your Confidence](https://www.talknative.io/confidence) | Short daily conversations with quiet corrections and a Confidence Score |
| [Start from Zero](https://www.talknative.io/start-from-zero) | A 60-day beginner course taught in the language you already speak |
| [Word Mode](https://www.talknative.io/word-mode) | Three new words a day, used out loud in your own sentences |

Every module has a free tier. Practice improves your chances in an exam or interview; it never guarantees a band score or a visa.
