TalkNative Speaking Practice extension: privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version

The extension collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. Nothing you type, select or record is sent to TalkNative.

What the extension does with your data

  • Text you select or type to hear. It is passed to Chrome’s built-in text-to-speech so it can be read aloud. Depending on the voice Chrome picks, Chrome may use Google’s online voices; that is handled by Chrome under Google’s privacy policy. The last text is kept in your browser’s local extension storage so it is still there next time you open the extension.
  • IELTS practice recordings. If you allow the microphone, your answer is recorded inside the practice tab so you can listen back. The recording is never uploaded or saved to disk, and it is deleted when you close the tab.
  • Notes. Notes you type during preparation stay on that screen and are not saved.
  • Settings. Your chosen language, speed and last open tab are kept in local extension storage on your device.

What it does not do

  • It does not read the pages you visit. It only receives text when you choose “Hear it spoken” from the right-click menu.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data, and does not use data for advertising or credit decisions.
  • It runs no remote code.

Links to TalkNative

Links in the extension open talknative.io with a tag that says the visit came from the extension. What happens on the website is covered by the TalkNative privacy policy.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored. You can also clear it at any time from Chrome’s extension settings.
